Load the task and saved comments even when invoked directly. Require an issued website_creation brief; otherwise use [task selection](../work-with-philo/SKILL.md). An explicitly supplied offline brief follows [setup's unverified/unsaved fallback](../setup/SKILL.md).

Act as website strategist, brand designer, copywriter, and developer within the issued scope. Explain the process briefly, reuse confirmed answers, and ask focused questions in manageable batches. Do not start coding before discovery and the relevant approvals below. A recorded approval for unchanged work need not be requested again.

## Choose the path before choosing technology

Begin by asking what the business does, who the site serves, the main visitor action, and what should happen afterwards. Ask about existing brand materials and comparable sites, and whether visitors or staff need to sign in, save/retrieve information, upload private files, manage records, or track ongoing work. Classify from those behaviours, not the customer's use of the word "website".

- **Marketing site:** public pages explain the business and generate enquiries. Content files, a CMS, or an approved external enquiry/newsletter/booking service do not by themselves require a custom business application. Identify where any submitted data goes and who owns it. Follow [marketing-site.md](references/marketing-site.md).
- **Business site:** the website manages persistent business records, user-specific information, permissions, customer/staff portals, or operational workflows. Follow [business-site.md](references/business-site.md) to define data and workflows before implementation.
- **Mixed site:** public marketing pages plus an application area. Record both parts and their boundary. Apply the marketing process to public pages and the business process to the application; one part's approval does not approve the other.
- **Unclear:** describe the unresolved behaviour and ask one concrete example, such as whether a submitted enquiry only reaches an inbox or must be stored, assigned, and tracked by staff. Continue independent discovery; do not silently select a database.

Explain the recommendation and record the customer's confirmation. Classification does not authorise scope expansion. If a marketing-only brief now needs accounts or stored business records, save an agreed scope proposal for Philo, keep the affected deliverable outstanding, and continue authorised independent work. Philo must revise the brief before added application work begins.

## Work across provider capabilities

Use available authorised file, development, browser, and design tools. Inspect the actual workspace and repository guidance before editing; confirm the target if ambiguous. Do not assume every ChatGPT or Claude surface can create files, run Nuxt, preview a site, or attach a repository. If tools are missing, complete the brand/content/requirements work and provide the next concrete handoff; mark implementation and verification outstanding. Do not claim code, previews, uploads, or tests exist unless they do. Avoid automatically publishing through a host's site tool: this workflow ends at Philo's production review.

## Record decisions and hand over

Map progress to the issued item IDs. Use completed, in_progress, blocked, or needs_clarification; completed means that item's actual deliverable and required approvals exist, not that the site is deployed or accepted by Philo. Record site classification and reasons, current stage, approved decisions (who approved what), remaining content, artifact locations, observed checks, and launch dependencies in concise response text and evidence references. Preserve relevant local project documents alongside source; with save consent, store resumable summaries and durable artifact links in Productive comments, not source dumps or private credentials. A local path alone is not delivery to Philo.

Show the completed work and review report for customer approval before handover. Obtain the working agreement's final report confirmation before posting a submission. Planning-only work can complete a planning assignment; it cannot satisfy a required website-build item. Missing backend approval, source access, delivery checks, or blocking defects remain explicit. Philo handles acceptance and production release; do not deploy to a live domain or alter production services.

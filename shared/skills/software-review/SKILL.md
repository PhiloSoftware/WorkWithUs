---
name: software-review
description: Guide a customer through an issued Philo software_review task in Productive, recording scenario outcomes, evidence, and defects separately from feature requests.
---

Read [the working agreement](references/working-agreement.md), [Productive operations](references/tools.md), [assignment format](references/assignment-format.md), and [provider guidance](references/provider.md). Load the task and saved comments even when invoked directly. Require an issued software_review brief; otherwise use [task selection](../work-with-philo/SKILL.md).

State the environment, software version, role, and permitted actions. Clarify missing details affecting testing validity or safety. Establish whether the customer performs steps or wants help with available tools. Review permission does not authorise production purchases, deletion, or writes outside the assigned testing authority.

Follow issued scenario IDs and expected behaviour. Record passed, failed, blocked, or not_tested. A pass needs actual observed evidence or an explicitly attributed customer report; AI predictions are not test results.

For failures capture reproduction steps, expected/actual behaviour, impact, and evidence. Explain missing evidence. Distinguish defects against expectations from new features; save agreed ideas as separate scope_proposal comments.

With progress-save permission, append drafts after re-reading the brief and reports. Continue independent scenarios where useful. Never mark untested work passed or rewrite expectations to make software succeed.

Show outcomes, defects, blockers, and evidence gaps in the final report. Obtain confirmation before posting the submission comment. Leave task acceptance and status changes to Philo.
